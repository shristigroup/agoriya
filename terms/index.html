How well tracking works

Tracking depends on your phone, GPS signal, network, battery and background-app settings, and some phones stop apps from reporting location. If TrackFolks stops receiving location for an extended period, it tries to wake the app and, if that fails, punches you out automatically and tells you and your manager. You can then punch in again.

Distances and routes are estimates built from GPS fixes and a third-party road-matching service, and can be wrong. Treat TrackFolks records as helpful information, not as a guaranteed or legally certified record. The organisation decides how to use them.
