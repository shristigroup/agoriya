Features

For field staff

Live route tracking, with replay

Your route is recorded in the background while you are punched in, and drawn on the map for you and your manager.

Selfie punch-in

Take a quick selfie when you punch in for verified attendance records.

Punched out by mistake? Resume

Resume your session instead of starting the day over.

Daily and monthly history

Every day's hours, distance and visits, plus monthly totals.

🏪

Customer visits and expenses

Log every client visit with location, notes and expense receipts.

For managers

Live team status

See who is at work, who has punched out and who has not started, and invite people with your organisation code.

Any team member's route and history

Open anyone's day to see their route, hours and distance, plus their monthly summary.

Export monthly reports

Download a day-wise CSV of punch times and distance for your whole team.

Privacy and control

🔒

Tracked only while punched in

Location is collected only between punch-in and punch-out, never outside your shift.

Delete your account anytime

Remove your account and all your data from the app in a few taps.

Transparent pricing that does not climb with your team

Most field tracking apps charge per user, so your bill keeps growing with every person you add. With TrackFolks the first 10 users are free, then it is $1 per user per month, and your bill stops at $399 a year however large the team gets, including beyond 100 users.

Some vendors also start with an introductory price that goes up later. Ours is published here upfront: capped at $199 in your first year and at $399 after that, with no base fee and no paid add-ons for GPS or route tracking. Data localisation, on-premise hosting and custom features are one-time costs; the usage cost stays at $399. There is nothing to find out at renewal.

Privacy first

Location is collected only while you are punched in. There is no tracking when staff are off duty.

Accurate distance measurement

Distance is measured along the route staff actually travelled, with stray GPS points filtered out and the route snapped to roads.

Easy on the battery

One location point a minute, uploaded every 15 minutes: as light as it can be while still showing managers near-real-time data, about 15 minutes behind.

Reliable

No crashes reported by the roughly 50 people who have used the app so far, from in-person feedback and TestFlight crash data.

Mobile first

Everything is done from the phone, including exporting CSV reports, so managers never need a dashboard to get work done.

Personal support from the creator

The creator answers on WhatsApp personally. Most teams need support only while onboarding, because the app is self-serve.

Try it yourself
