Features

For field staff

Live route tracking, with replay

Your route is recorded in the background while you are punched in, and drawn on the map for you and your manager.

Selfie punch-in

Take a quick selfie when you punch in for verified attendance records.

Punched out by mistake? Resume

Resume your session instead of starting the day over.

Daily and monthly history

Every day's hours, distance and visits, plus monthly totals.

🏪

Customer visits and expenses

Log every client visit with location, notes and expense receipts.

For managers

Live team status

See who is at work, who has punched out and who has not started, and invite people with your organisation code.

Any team member's route and history

Open anyone's day to see their route, hours and distance, plus their monthly summary.

Export monthly reports

Download a day-wise CSV of punch times and distance for your whole team.

Privacy and control

🔒

Tracked only while punched in

Location is collected only between punch-in and punch-out, never outside your shift.

Delete your account anytime

Remove your account and all your data from the app in a few taps.
