Alternative to Jobber for Field Service Management

Last checked November 2026 · Based on Google Play, App Store, G2, Capterra and Trustpilot reviews

People choose Jobber for what it does well, then leave it for a handful of problems. This page lists those problems, the ratings behind them and the field service management apps that do not share them.

What Jobber does well

  • Quoting, scheduling, invoicing and payments in one back office
  • Easy to use, with a large help centre and fast migration
  • Fast payment, automated reminders and growing features such as a pipeline and an AI receptionist

What people complain about

  • App stability on Android (63 bug mentions among 150 negative reviews)
  • Layout changes with updates and cost complaints (51 price mentions among negatives)
  • Technicians cannot clock in without signal
  • Routing is "as the crow flies"
  • The phone app is more limited than the web

Counts such as "14 of 46 negative reviews" are shares of the negative reviews read, not of all users. Google Play's "Most relevant" list leans negative, so these show what is common, not how many users are affected.

Better alternatives to Jobber

Each is in the same category and avoids a problem above. Where nothing avoids a problem, we suggest the highest rated app.

Zoho FSM

Google Play: 4.2 ★ (82) · App Store: 2.7 ★ (11) · plans of Rs 1,000, Rs 1,500 and Rs 1,750 a month by appointments, not per user; free plan for 30 appointments and 20 users

Price: Zoho FSM starts at Rs 1,000 a month with a free plan for 30 appointments, and is rated 4.2 on Google Play. Its iPhone app is weak (2.7 from 11 ratings). Its catch: the mobile app is the weak point: G2 says it needs an overhaul, the App Store lists an old, incomplete interface and an app that would not open on a new iPhone. (website)

Housecall Pro

Google Play: no listing found · App Store: 4.7 ★ (32,000) · Basic $59 a month for 1 user (no GPS), Essentials $149 for 5 users, Max $299 for 8 users, extra users $35

Mobile app and price per extra user: Housecall Pro is rated 4.7 on iPhone from 32K ratings, with extra users at $35. It has no listing in the India Play Store. Its catch: many features are add-ons, so the price climbs. (website)

When Jobber is still the right choice

Jobber fits small home-service crews; plans start at 1 user. Avoid it if technicians need to clock in without signal or you want routing beyond straight-line distance.
