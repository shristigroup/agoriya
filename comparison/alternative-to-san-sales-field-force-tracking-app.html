Alternative to SAN SALES (SANeFORCE) for Sales Force Automation

Last checked November 2026 · Based on Google Play, App Store, G2, Capterra and Trustpilot reviews

People choose SAN SALES (SANeFORCE) for what it does well, then leave it for a handful of problems. This page lists those problems, the ratings behind them and the sales force automation apps that do not share them.

What SAN SALES (SANeFORCE) does well

  • Attendance, order booking, retailing and call reporting for FMCG reps in one app (2022 Play review)
  • The vendor is shipping updates, with a release on 19 Nov 2026

What people complain about

  • August and September 2026 Play reviews say the app hangs, shows errors and is slow
  • Reviews of the sibling SAN SFE app complain about battery use and rep tracking
  • Very few iPhone ratings
  • No published price

Counts such as "14 of 46 negative reviews" are shares of the negative reviews read, not of all users. Google Play's "Most relevant" list leans negative, so these show what is common, not how many users are affected.

When SAN SALES (SANeFORCE) is still the right choice

SAN SALES (SANeFORCE) fits Indian FMCG teams that want a vendor with a long track record and are willing to pilot first. Avoid it if your reps use iPhones, or you need proof of stability.
