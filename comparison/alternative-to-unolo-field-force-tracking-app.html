Alternative to Unolo for Attendance and Field Force Tracking

Last checked November 2026 · Based on Google Play, App Store, G2, Capterra and Trustpilot reviews

People choose Unolo for what it does well, then leave it for a handful of problems. This page lists those problems, the ratings behind them and the field force automation apps that do not share them.

What Unolo does well

  • Easy to use
  • Accurate live tracking, timeline, attendance and reports
  • Supportive customer care

What people complain about

  • Distance can be off by 5 to 10% on Capterra and more on Google Play (28 of 116 negative reviews with text)
  • Battery drain
  • Gaps in leave approval, expense filters and record history (1 year)
  • Some users find it pricey

Battery drain and wrong distance counts are the top complaint about almost every field force app, and none is free of them. Test any app on one real route on your own phones before you commit.

Counts such as "14 of 46 negative reviews" are shares of the negative reviews read, not of all users. Google Play's "Most relevant" list leans negative, so these show what is common, not how many users are affected.

Better alternatives to Unolo

Each is in the same category and avoids a problem above. Where nothing avoids a problem, we suggest the highest rated app.

Lystloc

Google Play: 3.9 ★ (521) · App Store: 4.0 ★ (35) · about $3.67

iPhone rating: Lystloc is rated 4.0 on iPhone against Unolo's 3.3, though from only 35 ratings. Its catch: distance differences of 5 to 10 km and "as the crow flies" counts (14 of 46 negative Play reviews). (website)

dayTrack

Google Play: 4.1 ★ (868) · App Store: 4.2 ★ (22) · about $2.62 (plus a one-time setup fee of about $5.25 per user)

iPhone rating and price: dayTrack is rated 4.2 on iPhone, also from few ratings, and costs about $2.62. Its catch: battery drain (16 of 73 negative). (website)

TrackFolks

New listing, no public reviews yet · $1.05 per user per month, first 10 users free

Price: TrackFolks is $1.05 per user per month with the first 10 users free. It has no leave or shift management and no public reviews yet, so it fits teams that want core tracking at the lowest price.

When Unolo is still the right choice

Unolo fits mid-size Android-heavy teams that want the most proven app. Avoid it if your team is mostly on iPhone.
