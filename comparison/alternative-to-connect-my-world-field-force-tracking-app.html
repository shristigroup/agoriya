Alternatives to Connect My World for Attendance and Field Force Tracking

Last checked November 2026 · Based on Google Play, App Store, G2, Capterra and Trustpilot reviews

People choose Connect My World for what it does well, then leave it for a handful of problems. This page lists those problems, the ratings behind them and the field force automation apps that do not share them.

What Connect My World does well

  • Live tracking with point-by-point day replay, distance and kilometre logging
  • GPS or selfie attendance
  • Geofenced visits with arrival alerts and offline forms
  • All in one per-user price with no tiers

What people complain about

  • Battery drain
  • A phone that hangs or slows
  • "km not working properly" (a 2023 review)
  • Hard to uninstall because it runs as a device administrator (the vendor says background running is by design)
  • The Play reviews read (135) are mostly old, with only three from 2024 and 2025, and there are no Capterra or G2 reviews, so public evidence is thin
  • The iPhone apps show no ratings

Battery drain and wrong distance counts are the top complaint about almost every field force app, and none is free of them. Test any app on one real route on your own phones before you commit.

Counts such as "14 of 46 negative reviews" are shares of the negative reviews read, not of all users. Google Play's "Most relevant" list leans negative, so these show what is common, not how many users are affected.

Better alternatives to Connect My World

Each is in the same category and avoids a problem above. Where nothing avoids a problem, we suggest the highest rated app.

Unolo

Google Play: 4.7 ★ (6,470) · App Store: 3.3 ★ (59) · about $2.11 to $2.63

Recent evidence: Unolo is rated 4.7 on Google Play from 6.47K reviews, updated in November 2026, and starts at about $2.11. Its catch: distance can be off by 5 to 10% on Capterra and more on Google Play (28 of 116 negative reviews with text). (website)

dayTrack

Google Play: 4.1 ★ (868) · App Store: 4.2 ★ (22) · about $2.62 (plus a one-time setup fee of about $5.25 per user)

iPhone: dayTrack has iPhone ratings (4.2 from 22) and is rated 4.1 on Google Play from 868 reviews, at about $2.62. Its catch: battery drain (16 of 73 negative). (website)

Lystloc

Google Play: 3.9 ★ (521) · App Store: 4.0 ★ (35) · about $3.67

iPhone: Lystloc is rated 4.0 on iPhone and 3.9 on Google Play from 521 reviews, at about $3.67. Its catch: distance differences of 5 to 10 km and "as the crow flies" counts (14 of 46 negative Play reviews). (website)

When Connect My World is still the right choice

Connect My World fits small and mid-size Indian teams that want tracking, attendance and tasks at one low per-user price. Avoid it if your team is mostly on iPhone or you want recent public reviews before buying.
