Comparing the Top 11 Field Force Tracking Apps

Last checked October 2026 · 11 most-used attendance and location tracking apps, compared feature by feature

Attendance and field force tracking apps range from general time clocks that added GPS, to Indian attendance and payroll apps, to apps built for field staff. No app wins on every dimension. This guide compares the 11 most-used apps in the category on the features and prices that matter to a team on the road, and puts TrackFolks beside them. For what users complain about and which alternative suits each app, see Alternatives to the Top 11 Attendance and Field Force Tracking Apps.

How we picked the top 11

We looked at apps whose job is attendance and location tracking for people who work away from a desk, and ranked them by the number of ratings on Google Play and the App Store combined, because a count of ratings shows real usage better than a star score. Shift schedulers such as When I Work, Homebase and Deputy are left out because they record location only at clock-in, as are broader HR suites and sales force automation tools for consumer-goods distribution. TrackFolks is shown beside the 11 for comparison; its own ratings are too new to rank.

The factors that matter

These follow the columns of the table below, in the same order.

1Selfie / face check at punch-in

A selfie or face check shows who punched in, which reduces buddy punching. Check whether it is included in the plan you pay for.

2Live location

Live location answers "where is this person right now?" It is often an add-on or a top-tier feature, and usually works only while the person is punched in.

3Route history and distance

A recorded route answers "where did they go today, and how far?" It matters for mileage claims, proof of visits and beat compliance, and it needs continuous tracking.

4Geofencing

Geofencing restricts punches to a site, or punches people in and out automatically when they enter or leave one. It is usually a mid-tier feature.

5Customer visit logging

Field sales and service teams need to log each customer visit with a location, notes and photos. General time clocks often lack this.

6Expense claims

Expense claims, receipts and mileage reimbursement save the back-and-forth of paper bills.

7Leave / roster

HR-led businesses want leave balances and rosters in the same tool. Field-first apps often leave these out.

8Payroll

Built-in payroll is a big feature; many teams only need clean attendance data exported for their payroll tool.

9Works offline

With poor coverage, an app that cannot queue data loses it. Check what happens without a signal: some apps queue punches, others only queue locations.

10Biometric / QR attendance

Biometric devices, QR codes and kiosks suit fixed sites. Field teams mostly punch in on their own phones.

11Free plan

"Free" often means a trial, or a free plan without GPS. Look at the user limit and what is switched off.

12Price per user, year 1 and after renewal

Compare the price of the plan that includes the features you need, and what you pay in the first year against what you pay after renewal. Some vendors discount the first year or add fees later. Prices here are per user per month for a 30-user team, and ₹95 = $1.

13App store ratings

Store ratings are the most credible public signal of how well an app works. Look at the star rating and at the number of ratings: a high score from a handful of reviews means little.
