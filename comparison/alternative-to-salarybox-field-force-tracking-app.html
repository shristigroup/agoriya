If you are looking for an alternative, the most common reasons come down to renewal price increases that reviewers describe, plus a paid live-location add-on.

From the latest 200 Google Play and App Store reviews (September to October 2026 on Google Play, September 2025 to October 2026 on the App Store); about 16% were 1 to 3 stars. These are customers' opinions, paraphrased, and apps change, so check the latest reviews yourself.

1. Renewal price hikes and "not free"

2. App not opening after updates

about 3 in 10 of the low-star reviews

3. After-sale support and salary errors

about 1 in 7 of the low-star reviews
