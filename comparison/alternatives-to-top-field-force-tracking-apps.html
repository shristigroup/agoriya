Method: complaints are counted as the share of reviews that are 1 to 3 stars and mention the theme, by keyword and checked by reading. What the complaints add up to

  • Crashes and login errors show up in the reviews of 11 of 11 apps, most of all QuickBooks Time (37.5% of the reviews we read). TrackFolks: no crashes reported by the roughly 50 people who have used it so far.
  • Price and renewal complaints show up in the reviews of 6 of 11 apps, most of all PagarBook (5.5% of the reviews we read). TrackFolks: the price is published upfront and capped at $499 a year ($249 in the first year).
  • Always-on or off-duty location complaints show up in the reviews of 3 of 11 apps, most of all Connecteam (5.5% of the reviews we read). TrackFolks: location is collected only while you are punched in.
  • Battery drain complaints show up in the reviews of 5 of 11 apps, most of all FieldSense (11.6% of the reviews we read). TrackFolks: one location point a minute, uploaded every 15 minutes: as light as it can be while still giving near-real-time data.
  • Distance and GPS accuracy complaints show up in the reviews of 4 of 11 apps, most of all FieldSense (13.2% of the reviews we read). TrackFolks: distance is measured along the route staff travelled, with stray GPS points removed.
  • Support complaints show up in the reviews of 6 of 11 apps, most of all PagarBook (8.5% of the reviews we read). TrackFolks: the creator gives personal support, and most teams need it only while onboarding.
